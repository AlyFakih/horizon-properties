Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our services

What sets Horizon Properties apart from other real estate agencies?

Horizon Properties distinguishes itself through our commitment to personalized service, deep market expertise, and luxury focus. Our agents have specialized knowledge in high-end properties and provide concierge-level service tailored to each client's unique needs. We leverage cutting-edge technology and marketing strategies while maintaining the personal touch that defines exceptional service.

How long does it typically take to sell a property with Horizon Properties?

While market conditions vary, our premium properties typically sell within 30-60 days, compared to the market average of 90+ days. Our strategic pricing, professional marketing, and targeted exposure to qualified buyers help achieve faster sales without sacrificing value. During your initial consultation, we'll provide a more specific timeline based on your property's unique characteristics and current market conditions.

What is included in your property management services?

Our comprehensive property management services include tenant screening and selection, rent collection, regular property inspections, maintenance coordination, financial reporting, and 24/7 emergency response. We handle all tenant communications and ensure legal compliance with local regulations. Property owners receive monthly statements and have access to our online portal for real-time updates on their investment.

How do you determine the right price for my property?

We conduct a thorough Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) that evaluates recent sales of similar properties, current market conditions, your property's unique features, and location-specific factors. Our luxury market specialists are skilled in identifying the subtle details that add value to premium properties. We combine this analysis with our understanding of buyer psychology to recommend a strategic price that maximizes both value and market interest.

Can you help with international property investments?

Yes, Horizon Properties has extensive experience with international real estate investments. Through our global network of partners, we can help you identify opportunities worldwide, navigate foreign real estate regulations, manage currency considerations, and structure investments optimally. Our investment consulting team includes specialists in international tax implications and cross-border transactions to ensure a smooth process regardless of location.

What should I do to prepare my home for selling?

Our team will provide personalized recommendations during our initial assessment, but generally we advise: decluttering to showcase space, making minor repairs, enhancing curb appeal, deep cleaning, and considering professional staging. With our Premium and Luxury packages, we coordinate many of these preparations for you, including professional staging that can significantly increase perceived value and reduce time on market.
