Our Story

A legacy of excellence in real estate since 2005.

Horizon Properties was founded by Jonathan and Elizabeth Horizon with a vision to transform the luxury real estate market by focusing on personalized service, uncompromising quality, and a deep understanding of client needs.

What began as a boutique agency with just five dedicated agents has grown into a premier real estate company with offices across the country, but our founding principles remain unchanged. We believe in building lasting relationships with our clients by providing exceptional service and unparalleled market expertise.

Today, we're proud to be recognized as industry leaders in luxury real estate, having facilitated over $5 billion in property transactions while maintaining the personalized attention and dedication that set us apart from the beginning.

Our Mission

To connect discerning clients with exceptional properties while providing an unparalleled real estate experience defined by integrity, expertise, and personalized service.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and respected name in luxury real estate, setting the standard for excellence in every market we serve.

Our Values

  • Integrity - We conduct business with unwavering honesty and transparency.
  • Excellence - We strive for perfection in every aspect of our service.
  • Innovation - We embrace new technologies and approaches to better serve our clients.
  • Relationship - We value long-term client relationships above short-term gains.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the Horizon Properties story

2005

Foundation

Horizon Properties founded in New York City by Jonathan and Elizabeth Horizon.

First Expansion

$1 Billion Milestone

Surpassed $1 billion in total property transactions.

International Launch

Established our first international partnership, expanding our services to Europe.

2025

20th Anniversary

Celebrating two decades of excellence with offices in 15 major cities worldwide.
